Native DCT Coefficient Ordering

Operates directly in the quantized frequency domain using jpeglib. Avoids lossy RGB round-trips and spatial rounding artifacts entirely.

Authenticated Encryption

Scrypt memory-hard key derivation paired with AES-256-GCM. Guarantees binary authentication and eliminates false-positive plaintext recovery.

Reed-Solomon RS(48,32) ECC

Injects 16 parity bytes per 32 data bytes, allowing autonomous healing of up to 8 symbol errors per codeword caused by compression noise.
